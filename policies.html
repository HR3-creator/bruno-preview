Shipping

Every piece is handmade and one of one, packed by Bruno in Queens, NY.

Where and how much

OptionCost

Hats ship by USPS with tracking. Shipping is United States only for now. If you are outside the US, send a message and Bruno will work out a quote.

When

  • Orders ship within 5 business days of payment. Tracking is emailed when your order ships.
  • USPS usually delivers within a few business days of shipping. Delivery dates are USPS estimates, not promises.
  • If Bruno cannot ship in time, you will get an email with a new date and a free, easy way to cancel for a full refund. Nothing ships late without your OK.

Meet-ups

After your order, Bruno reaches out to set a time and a public spot in NYC. Please bring your order confirmation.

Lost or damaged in transit

Tell us within 7 days of delivery (or of the expected delivery date if it never arrives). Bruno will file the carrier claim and refund or replace the piece where possible. Because every piece is one of one, a replacement may not be possible and a full refund is the fallback.

Privacy

What we collect

Only what you type when you order: your name, email, phone (optional), shipping address and any note to Bruno. Your cart is saved in your own browser and is not sent anywhere until you place an order.

How we use it

  • To fill your order, ship it, and contact you about it.
  • To keep the records that tax and accounting rules require.
  • We do not sell your information and we do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.

Who else sees it

  • Our payment processor, when card payments are switched on. Bruno never sees or stores your full card number.
  • The shipping carrier, which gets your name and address.
  • Tax or legal authorities, only if the law requires it.

Things to know

  • This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page opens.
  • Links to Instagram take you to a site with its own privacy policy.
  • This site is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, message Bruno: on Instagram @Bruno.Saquicela. Some order records must be kept for tax reasons, but we will delete what we are allowed to.

Terms

One of one

Each piece is a single original and handmade, so small differences from the photos, such as the placement of studs, beads and patches, are part of the work. The first paid order gets it. If a piece is sold in person before your order is confirmed, we will cancel and refund you in full.

Prices and sales tax

Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax is not charged on hats priced under $110, which New York State and New York City exempt as clothing, and shipping that is listed separately is exempt along with them. If tax applies to another piece, it is shown before you pay.

About the photos

Photos are of the real pieces. Some "clean views" have the background removed, and on some hats the clean view is a digital front-on recreation made from Bruno's photo so the whole hat can be shown. The "Original photo" on each item page is the unedited photo of the real hat. Colors vary by screen.

Safety

Hats and some other work have small beads, studs and charms. They are adult art and fashion pieces, not toys. Keep them away from children under 3 and check that everything is secure before wearing.
